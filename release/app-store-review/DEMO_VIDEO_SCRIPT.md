# Demo Video Script — IndexBoost SEO

**Target length:** 2–3 minutes  
**Audience:** Shopify merchants and App Store reviewers  
**Format:** Screen recording of Shopify Admin with voiceover

---

## Scene 1 — Problem Statement (0:00–0:20)

**Screen:** Shopify Admin > Products list  
**Voiceover:**
> "When you publish new products or update pages in your Shopify store, search engines like Google don't know immediately. IndexBoost SEO fixes that by automatically notifying Google and other search engines the moment you make a change — so your content gets indexed faster."

---

## Scene 2 — App Install & Dashboard (0:20–0:45)

**Screen:** App install OAuth screen → App Dashboard  
**Action:** Show the main dashboard with submission stats, quota bar, and status indicators  
**Voiceover:**
> "After installing IndexBoost SEO, you land on the dashboard. You can see your daily Google submission quota, IndexNow status, and a quick summary of recent submissions. Auto-indexing is on by default."

---

## Scene 3 — Auto-Indexing in Action (0:45–1:10)

**Screen:** Shopify Admin > Products > Edit a product > Save. Then switch to IndexBoost > Submission Logs  
**Action:** Show a new log entry appearing after the product save  
**Voiceover:**
> "IndexBoost listens for Shopify webhooks. The moment you save a product update, the URL is automatically queued and submitted to Google and Bing within seconds. No manual action needed."

---

## Scene 4 — AI SEO Optimizer (1:10–1:45)

**Screen:** IndexBoost > AI SEO Optimizer  
**Action:** Select a product with a low SEO score → click Generate → show preview → click Apply  
**Voiceover:**
> "The AI SEO Optimizer uses Claude AI to write SEO-optimized titles and meta descriptions for your products. Select products with low scores, click Generate, review the suggestions, and apply them with one click. Credits are shown in the top right."

---

## Scene 5 — Meta Tags & Redirects (1:45–2:10)

**Screen:** IndexBoost > Meta Tags Editor → then Redirect Manager  
**Action:** Edit a product's SEO title in the modal → save. Then show adding a redirect.  
**Voiceover:**
> "The Meta Tags Editor lets you edit SEO titles and descriptions for products, collections, and pages in one place. And the Redirect Manager lets you create 301 redirects to preserve SEO equity when URLs change."

---

## Scene 6 — Plans & Billing (2:10–2:30)

**Screen:** IndexBoost > Plans & Billing  
**Action:** Show the three plan cards  
**Voiceover:**
> "IndexBoost SEO has three plans. Free gives you 50 Google submissions per day plus unlimited IndexNow. Pro adds bulk tools, the meta editor, sitemaps, and more. Business adds AI SEO and image optimization. Upgrade directly inside the app — billed through Shopify, no extra accounts needed."

---

## Scene 7 — Call to Action (2:30–2:45)

**Screen:** App Store listing page  
**Voiceover:**
> "IndexBoost SEO — automatic, intelligent URL indexing for your Shopify store. Start free today."

---

## Recording Notes

- Use a store with real products (at least 10 products with varied content)
- Ensure AI credits are available for Scene 4 (reset credits if needed for demo)
- Mute notification sounds from the browser
- Crop out browser address bar and OS taskbar
- Export at 1080p minimum, 30fps
