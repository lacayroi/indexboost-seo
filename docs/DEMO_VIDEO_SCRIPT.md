# IndexBoost SEO - Demo Video Script

Target length: 60-90 seconds.

## 0:00-0:08 - Open App

Visual: Open IndexBoost SEO inside Shopify Admin.

Voiceover:

"IndexBoost SEO helps Shopify stores send indexing notifications for important URLs, without adding heavy storefront scripts or modifying your theme."

## 0:08-0:20 - Onboarding

Visual: Show dashboard onboarding and setup checklist.

Voiceover:

"When you install the app, the dashboard guides you through setup steps: connect Google Indexing API if you have eligible URLs and credentials, submit your first URL, and enable automatic submissions."

## 0:20-0:32 - Engine Status

Visual: Show Engines card with Google and IndexNow status.

Voiceover:

"You can see Google and IndexNow status at a glance. Google requires your own Service Account credentials, Search Console access, and eligible URL types under Google's current rules. IndexNow is generated automatically."

## 0:32-0:44 - Submit URL

Visual: Open Submit URLs page and submit one product URL.

Voiceover:

"Use manual submission for important products, collections, pages, or blog posts. The app also supports queued submissions when Shopify content changes."

## 0:44-0:54 - View Logs

Visual: Show Recent Submissions or Logs page.

Voiceover:

"Submission logs show the engine, status, content type, and any errors returned by Google or IndexNow. Indexing and ranking are not guaranteed, but you always know what was submitted."

## 0:54-1:06 - Upgrade Plan

Visual: Open Plans page with Free, Pro, and Business.

Voiceover:

"The Free plan includes basic submission tools. Pro unlocks bulk submissions and technical SEO tools. Business adds image SEO, alerts, and index health views."

## 1:06-1:18 - Pro SEO Tools

Visual: Show SEO Audit, Redirects, Broken Links, or LLMs.txt.

Voiceover:

"Pro tools help you review SEO issues, manage redirects, scan broken links, generate schema, and create LLMs.txt for AI search visibility."

## 1:18-1:28 - Business Image SEO Teaser

Visual: Show Image SEO screen.

Voiceover:

"Business tools help improve image SEO with smarter alt text workflows and monitoring for stores that publish frequently."

## 1:28-1:30 - Close

Visual: Return to dashboard.

Voiceover:

"IndexBoost SEO is a lightweight way to manage indexing and practical technical SEO from Shopify Admin."
